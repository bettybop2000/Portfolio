Overview

The Concept

BettybopAnimation Show is a 2D animated talk show format built around a single character — Bettybop2000. Think Daria meets Downtown meets the TikTok creator who just talks directly at the camera about whatever is on their mind. Random thoughts. Pressing issues. Delivered with dry, sardonic humour that makes you laugh before you've realised what happened.

The show lives at the intersection of retro and futuristic — a visual language that feels like it was made in another era but couldn't exist without now. Every episode is a one-person performance. No guests. No sets. Just the character, the viewer, and whatever Bettybop has decided is worth saying today.

The Brief

Creative Direction

The creative challenge was building a character strong enough to carry a show alone. No supporting cast. No recurring plot. Just a personality so distinct that people come back for the next episode not because of what happens but because of who is speaking.

Inspiration came from Total Drama Island's bold character design — strong line work, expressive features, silhouettes that read instantly. Daria and MTV's Downtown shaped the tone — smart, deadpan, never trying too hard. The kind of humour that respects the audience's intelligence.

The retro-futuristic aesthetic was a deliberate choice — it signals that this show exists outside of any particular moment. Not nostalgic. Not trend-chasing. Timeless in its weirdness.

"A single character. Direct to camera. Whatever Bettybop has decided matters today — random thoughts, pressing issues, delivered with the kind of humour that makes you feel like you're in on something."

Character Design

Bettybop2000

The character was designed from the ground up in Procreate. Strong clean line work was the foundation — every feature drawn with intention, nothing accidental. Smooth curves balanced by sharp details. A silhouette that reads at thumbnail size on a phone screen.

The retro-futuristic styling runs through everything — the proportions, the colour palette, the way the character holds themselves. There is an oddball quality to the design that is entirely deliberate. Bettybop2000 is not trying to be relatable. She is trying to be undeniable.

Process

How It Was Made

Every episode was created entirely in Procreate — drawn frame by frame, exported and edited in CapCut. The animation style prioritises expressiveness over technical complexity. Smooth features, strong mouth and eye movement, enough motion to feel alive without losing the hand-drawn quality that makes it distinctive.

The talk show format was chosen specifically because it is the most honest format for what the show is — one person with something to say. It strips away everything unnecessary and puts the character and the writing at the centre. If those two things work, the show works.

Social content was developed alongside the animation — stills, character assets, promotional graphics — all designed to perform on TikTok where the audience actually lives.
